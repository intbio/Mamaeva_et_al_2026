b Induction in BPCL is achieved through covalent click chemistry (SPAAC/SPIEDAC), in contrast to the equilibrium chemical/optical induction of CLOuD9 and LADL. Reversal therefore requires an active UV signal rather than simple inducer removal. For dCas9-Zips and dgRNA, induction/reversal has not been demonstrated in the primary publications but could in principle be achieved by combining these architectures with switchable dCas systems.

c The 167 kb value comes from Wang et al. 2021 (doi: 10.1016/j.stem.2021.04.023), who applied CLOuD9 to fuse two TADs flanking the Dppa5a locus in mouse MEFs.
